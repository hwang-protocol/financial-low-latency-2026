	DeviceName: pciPassthru0
	Subsystem: Solarflare Communications XtremeScale X2522-25G Network Adapter [1924:8028]
	Kernel driver in use: sfc
	Kernel modules: sfc

[支持文档] DPDK 21.11.9 → Solarflare libefx-based PMD → Supported NICs

该节列出 Solarflare X2522 Dual Port SFP28 10/25GbE Adapter。
